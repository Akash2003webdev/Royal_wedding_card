# Royal Wedding Cards SEO deployment checklist

1. Deploy to `https://royalcardsindia.com` and set it as the primary domain.
2. Confirm `/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`, `/logo-512.png`, `/og-image.jpg`, and `/site.webmanifest` return HTTP 200.
3. Verify the domain in Google Search Console and submit `https://royalcardsindia.com/sitemap.xml`.
4. Request indexing for Home, Collections, Gallery, About and Contact.
5. The official Royal Bags & Wedding Cards logo is already connected to the header, schema, favicon, app icons and social preview.
6. Keep phone, address, opening hours and business name identical on the website and Google Business Profile.
7. Add verified Google Maps, Instagram and Facebook URLs to structured data `sameAs` only after confirming exact public links.
8. Product/category URLs are added to the sitemap automatically during deployment when Vercel Supabase environment variables are available.

Search position cannot be guaranteed. Content quality, local relevance, site speed, genuine reviews, Google Business Profile activity and backlinks affect ranking.
